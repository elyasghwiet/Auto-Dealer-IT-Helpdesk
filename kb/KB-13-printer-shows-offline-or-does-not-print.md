# KB-13: Printer shows Offline or does not print

**Category:** Printers and Scanners  |  **Applies to:** Windows 11 and network printers

## The problem
The printer is on, but your document stays in the queue or the printer says Offline.

## Fix it yourself
1. Check the printer screen for paper jams, empty paper or toner messages.
2. Open Settings, Bluetooth & devices, Printers & scanners, choose the printer, then Open print queue. Cancel stuck jobs.
3. Restart the printer and wait two minutes.
4. Ask a colleague if they can print. If yes, only your computer has the problem.
5. Remove the printer and add it again by its IP address (ask the Service Desk for the address).

**[SCREENSHOT]** The Printers & scanners settings page and the print queue.

## Still not working?
If nobody can print, the printer's IP address may have changed; report it as a printer ticket.

## How to avoid it
Printers have a fixed IP reserved in the router so the address does not change.

## Open a ticket
Category and priority: Printers and Scanners, Low (Medium if the department cannot print at all)
