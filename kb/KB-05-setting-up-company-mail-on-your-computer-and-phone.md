# KB-05: Setting up company mail on your computer and phone

**Category:** Email  |  **Applies to:** Outlook on Windows 11; iPhone and Android

## The problem
You are new, or you got a new phone, and need company mail.

## Fix it yourself
1. On the computer: open Outlook, choose Add account, type your company e-mail address, then enter your password and MFA code.
2. On the phone: open Settings, Mail (or Accounts), Add account, choose Microsoft 365 or Exchange, and sign in.
3. If the phone says it cannot verify the server identity, check that the date and time are set to automatic.
4. Send yourself a test e-mail and check it arrives on both devices.
5. Do not install other mail apps that ask for your password.

**[SCREENSHOT]** Outlook 'Add account' window; phone Accounts screen.

## Still not working?
If Outlook keeps asking for a password, open Control Panel, Credential Manager, Windows Credentials, remove the old Office entries, and restart Outlook.

## How to avoid it
Keep the phone's date and time on automatic.
