# KB-03: How to connect to the company VPN

**Category:** Network and VPN  |  **Applies to:** Windows 11, from home or travelling

## The problem
You need to reach company files or applications from outside the office.

## Fix it yourself
1. Check that your home internet works: open any website.
2. Open the VPN app from the Start menu and choose the profile 'EV-Fleet-VPN'.
3. Enter your company username and password, then approve the MFA prompt on your phone.
4. Wait for the status 'Connected', then open the shared folder or application.
5. When finished, press Disconnect.

**[SCREENSHOT]** The VPN app with 'Connected' status.

## Still not working?
If it says 'connection timed out': try a mobile hotspot. If the hotspot works, your home router blocks the VPN; ask us to switch your profile to TCP 443 (see KB-04).

## How to avoid it
Keep the VPN app updated. Do not use free VPN apps on company laptops.
