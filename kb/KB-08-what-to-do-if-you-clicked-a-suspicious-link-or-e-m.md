# KB-08: What to do if you clicked a suspicious link or e-mail

**Category:** Security Incident  |  **Applies to:** All employees

## The problem
You opened an e-mail link or file and you are not sure it was safe, or you typed your password on a strange page.

## Fix it yourself
1. Do not panic and do not delete anything.
2. Disconnect the computer from the network (turn Wi-Fi off or unplug the cable).
3. Call the Service Desk immediately. Say: 'I may have clicked a phishing link.'
4. Do not keep using the same password anywhere. We will reset your password and check your account.
5. Forward the e-mail as an attachment to the security mailbox so we can block it for everyone.

**[SCREENSHOT]** An example phishing e-mail with the sender address highlighted (use a harmless training sample).

## Still not working?
If a message asks for money or gift cards, tell us even if you did not click.

## How to avoid it
Check the sender address, hover over links, and never enter your password after clicking a link in an e-mail.

## Open a ticket
Category and priority: Security Incident, Urgent
