# KB-06: Your laptop is slow: five quick checks

**Category:** Hardware (Laptops and Desktops)  |  **Applies to:** Windows 11

## The problem
The laptop takes minutes to start or freezes while working.

## Fix it yourself
1. Restart the laptop. A laptop that has not been restarted for weeks gets slow.
2. Press Ctrl+Shift+Esc to open Task Manager. Look at the CPU, Memory and Disk columns. If one is near 100%, note which app uses it.
3. Open the 'Startup apps' tab and disable apps you do not need at start (not antivirus or VPN).
4. Open Settings, Windows Update and install pending updates. Restart.
5. Open Settings, System, Storage, Temporary files and remove temporary files. Keep at least 15% of the disk free.

## Still not working?
If the disk is always at 100% or the battery drains fast, open a ticket. We will check the disk health and battery report.

## How to avoid it
Restart weekly. Close browser tabs you are not using.
