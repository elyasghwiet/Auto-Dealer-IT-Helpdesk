# KB-01: How to reset a forgotten Windows password

**Category:** Accounts and Access  |  **Applies to:** Windows 11 laptops and desktops

## The problem
You cannot sign in because you forgot your password or it has expired.

## Fix it yourself
1. On the sign-in screen, check that Caps Lock is off and the keyboard language is English.
2. If your password has expired, sign-in will ask you to create a new one. Choose at least 12 characters.
3. If you forgot it, call the Service Desk or send a ticket from your phone. We will confirm your identity with your employee ID.
4. The technician sets a temporary password. Sign in with it and Windows will ask you to choose a new one.
5. Sign out and sign in again with the new password. Update the password on your phone mail app too (see KB-05).

**[SCREENSHOT]** The Windows sign-in screen with the 'Reset' message; the 'Change a password' screen.

## Still not working?
If the account says 'locked', read KB-02. If it says 'account disabled', contact your manager.

## How to avoid it
Use a long phrase you can remember. Never write the password on a sticky note. Do not reuse it for other websites.
