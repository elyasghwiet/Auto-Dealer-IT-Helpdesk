# KB-07: Wi-Fi does not connect or keeps dropping

**Category:** Network and VPN  |  **Applies to:** Windows 11, handheld scanners

## The problem
The laptop shows 'No internet' or the signal drops.

## Fix it yourself
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on, or use Airplane mode on and off.
2. Check other devices: if they also fail, tell the Service Desk; the office internet may be down.
3. Open Settings, Network & internet, Wi-Fi, choose the network, and select Forget. Connect again with the Wi-Fi password.
4. Move closer to the access point. In the garage and charging area the signal is weaker.
5. Restart the laptop.

**[SCREENSHOT]** The Wi-Fi settings page with the Forget button.

## Still not working?
If one place always drops, send us the room name. We will check the signal strength and may move an access point.

## How to avoid it
Do not connect company devices to public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
