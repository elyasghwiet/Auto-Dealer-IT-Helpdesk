# KB-17: Teams camera, microphone or headset not working

**Category:** Software and Licences  |  **Applies to:** Microsoft Teams on Windows 11

## The problem
Others cannot see or hear you in a meeting.

## Fix it yourself
1. Open Teams, Settings, Devices, and choose the correct camera, microphone and speaker.
2. Check the Windows privacy settings: Settings, Privacy & security, Camera / Microphone, and allow apps.
3. Unplug and plug the USB headset into another port.
4. Restart Teams and test again with 'Make a test call'.
5. If still not working, update the audio/camera driver from Device Manager.

**[SCREENSHOT]** Teams Devices settings.

## Still not working?
If the device does not appear in Device Manager, it may be broken; we will replace it.

## How to avoid it
Test your equipment five minutes before important meetings.
