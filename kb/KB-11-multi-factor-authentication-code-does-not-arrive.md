# KB-11: Multi-factor authentication code does not arrive

**Category:** Accounts and Access  |  **Applies to:** Authenticator app on iPhone and Android

## The problem
You are asked for an MFA code or approval but nothing arrives.

## Fix it yourself
1. Check that phone notifications are enabled for the Authenticator app.
2. Open the app by hand; the code may be inside it even if no notification came.
3. Check the phone has internet and that date and time are automatic.
4. If you changed phones, you must register the app again; contact the Service Desk.
5. Never share the code with anyone, even someone who says they are from IT.

**[SCREENSHOT]** The Authenticator notification settings.

## Still not working?
If you lost your phone, call us immediately so we can protect your account.

## How to avoid it
Keep a second sign-in method registered, such as a work phone number.

## Open a ticket
Category and priority: Accounts and Access, Medium (High if the phone is lost)
