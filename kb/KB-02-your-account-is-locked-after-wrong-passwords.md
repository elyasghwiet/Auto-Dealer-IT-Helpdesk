# KB-02: Your account is locked after wrong passwords

**Category:** Accounts and Access  |  **Applies to:** Windows accounts, phones with company mail

## The problem
You see 'The referenced account is currently locked out'.

## Fix it yourself
1. Wait 15 minutes; the account unlocks by itself.
2. Think about where an old password is still saved: your phone mail app, Wi-Fi settings, mapped drives, a second laptop.
3. Update the password in those places, or remove the old account from the phone.
4. If you cannot wait, ask the Service Desk to unlock the account. We will also tell you which device caused the lockout.

**[SCREENSHOT]** The lockout message; phone 'Accounts' settings screen.

## Still not working?
If the account locks again within an hour, a device is still using the old password. Open a ticket and give us the device names.

## How to avoid it
After every password change, update the phone mail app first.
